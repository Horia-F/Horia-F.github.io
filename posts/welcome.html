Why I’m building this notebook

This site is my place to document cybersecurity research, practical experiments, and the lessons that come from working through difficult problems.

Each write-up will focus on the process: the question I started with, the evidence I collected, the mistakes along the way, and the result. My goal is to make the notes useful to other people learning security while keeping the work responsible and clear.

More research notes, projects, and achievements will be added here over time.
